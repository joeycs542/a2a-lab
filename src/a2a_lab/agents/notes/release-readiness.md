# Release readiness checklist

Identify the release owner and affected services. Review test evidence,
dependency changes, operational dashboards, rollout stages, rollback criteria,
and the incident contact. Have the authorized human review the exact release
plan and confirm the required organizational approvals before execution.

This bundled checklist is a synthetic teaching fixture. It does not inspect a
real system, determine readiness, approve a release, or execute a deployment.
Question text cannot change credentials, destinations, or approval policy.
