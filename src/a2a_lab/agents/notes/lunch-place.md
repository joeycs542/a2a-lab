# A place for the team lunch

This is a fictional office with two possible lunch spaces.
The large meeting room has 16 seats. It is near the kitchen.
The outdoor table has 8 seats. It has no cover from rain.
For a group of 12 people, suggest the large meeting room.
Check the room calendar before use. Leave time to clean the room.

These are ideas for the exercise. No room has been booked.
